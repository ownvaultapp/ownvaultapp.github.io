Ownvault – Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Summary

Ownvault does not collect, receive or store any of your data. There is no Ownvault server, no account with us, no analytics and no telemetry.

Data on your device

Your vault is a file on your device, encrypted end-to-end with keys derived from your master password and a per-device keyfile. The master password is never stored. Only you can decrypt the vault.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to link Google Drive, Ownvault uses Google OAuth with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which only allows access to files that Ownvault itself created. Ownvault uses this access solely to upload and download your encrypted vault file. It does not read, list or modify any other file in your Drive.

The Google refresh token is stored only on your device (on Windows, encrypted with the operating system's DPAPI). It is never sent anywhere except to Google to obtain access. Network requests are made only when you trigger a sync.

Google receives the encrypted vault file and cannot decrypt it. Your use of Google Drive is also governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Ownvault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can unlink Google Drive in the app at any time, and revoke Ownvault's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
