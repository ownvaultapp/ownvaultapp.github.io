Ownvault

A zero-knowledge, serverless password manager.

Ownvault keeps your passwords in a single file, encrypted end-to-end on your device (Argon2id, XChaCha20-Poly1305). There is no Ownvault server and no telemetry.

Optionally, you can link your own Google Drive to keep a copy of the encrypted vault file in sync between your devices. Ownvault only accesses the files it created itself (Google scope drive.file); Google only ever sees the encrypted file.

Available for Windows, Linux and Android.
